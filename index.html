When Status is GOOD
  • Continue regular ETP (Effluent Treatment Plant) operations
  • Maintain green buffer zones around industrial areas
  • Conduct monthly calibration of all IoT sensors
  • Submit compliance reports to GPCB proactively
  • Train workers on environmental best practices
  • Document pollution levels for historical analysis
⚠️
When Status is MODERATE
  • Increase monitoring frequency to every 5 minutes
  • Inspect and optimize industrial scrubbers
  • Reduce load on high-emission production lines
  • Alert shift supervisors for immediate review
  • Check ETP for efficiency — replace filter media if needed
  • Issue precautionary advisory to nearby communities
🚨
When Status is BAD / Critical
  • Immediately halt or reduce production at affected units
  • Notify GPCB (Gujarat Pollution Control Board) within 1 hour
  • Activate emergency ventilation and containment systems
  • Evacuate personnel from high-risk zones
  • Issue public health warning for nearby communities
  • Deploy rapid response team for source identification

🤖 AI Agents
📡 Emission & Effluent Monitoring Agent

Continuously collects data from IoT sensors deployed across all three industrial zones. Monitors 8 air parameters (PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NO2, CO, O3, VOC, H2S) and 8 water parameters (pH, BOD, COD, TDS, Turbidity, DO, Nitrates, Heavy Metals) in real-time every 5 seconds.

⚖️ Violation Detection & Compliance Agent

Compares measured values against CPCB/GPCB regulatory thresholds. Automatically classifies each parameter as Good / Moderate / Bad. Calculates AQI and WQI composite scores. Flags violations instantly.

📢 Regulatory Alert & Escalation Agent

Generates prioritized alerts based on severity. Triggers escalation protocols for critical violations. Notifies regulatory authorities (GPCB) automatically. Maintains audit trail of all alerts for legal compliance.

🏥 Public Health Risk Assessment Agent

Evaluates combined pollution exposure risk for nearby communities. Uses IBM Granite LLM to generate contextual health advisories. Provides evacuation recommendations for critical scenarios.

📊 Industrial Pollution Dashboard Agent

Aggregates and visualizes all sensor data in real-time. Generates trend charts, status summaries, and comparative analytics across Vapi, Ankleshwar, and Vatva. Powers the unified monitoring dashboard.
